## Editorial and source rules

Use accessible primary or authoritative sources; verify claims and URLs. Use EdUHK Library only when institutional access materially helps, prefer an existing authorized session, never persist or expose credentials. Differentiate narrow new mechanisms from the existing 70 broad introductory lessons. Do not present contested theories or correlational findings as universal causal laws. No invented study results, testimonials, publication history, or guarantees.
